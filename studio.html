Studio

Make a beat, write a melody and mix it all together, right in your browser.

Works best on a laptop or Chromebook with headphones.

Open full screen

Build a beat

Pick the Drums track and click the grid to add hits. Drag across a row to paint a pattern, then press Repeat bar 1 to loop it.

Write a melody

Pick an instrument track to open the piano roll. Click to add a note, drag to make it longer, and drag a note to move it.

Mix it

Use Vol and Pan on each track. M mutes a track and S lets you hear it on its own. The space bar plays and stops.

Save and hand in

Copy song link puts your whole song in a link you can paste into Classroom or Teams. Export WAV for audio, or MIDI to keep working in another app.
