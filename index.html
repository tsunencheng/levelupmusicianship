For warm-ups, brain-breaks and early finishers.

Every game on this site started on paper in my classroom. My students would tap and point their way through circles and hand shapes on an A4 sheet, and once an activity worked, I turned it into a game so they could keep practising on their own.

Every title has several levels, so a whole class can play together, each student at the difficulty that suits them.

The Activities page has a variety of games that can be played as a class or in groups.
