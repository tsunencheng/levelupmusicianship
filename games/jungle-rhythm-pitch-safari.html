Jungle Rhythm& Pitch Safari

Listen, then tap it back on the right notes. Starts simple, and more notes join as you climb!

Rhythms: ta, ti-ti. Pitches: do, re, mi, then so and la join in later levels. Best on a computer keyboard.

For teachers

What students practise

This adds solfa to the original Jungle Rhythm Safari. Students tap the rhythm and enter do, re, mi, so and la on the number keys, and the pentatonic set grows one note at a time across the five levels.

Using it in class

Sing the new note with hand signs as a class before each level. It works well straight after Jungle Rhythm Safari, once tapping rhythms back feels easy.
