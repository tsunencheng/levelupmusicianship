Jungle Rhythm Safari · after dark

NightDictation

Listen closely in the dark, then tap back what you heard, by ear alone.

Rhythms: ta, ti-ti, tika-tika, ti-tika, tika-ti, too-oo. Best on a computer keyboard: tap SPACE in time with the beat. You won't see the rhythm while it plays, so trust your ears!

For teachers

What students practise

The same jungle rhythms, but nothing is shown while the pattern plays. Students have to hold the rhythm in their head before they tap it back or write it down.

Using it in class

Save this one for after students are confident with the original Jungle Rhythm Safari. You can pair it with mini whiteboards so students write the rhythm down first, then tap it in to check.
