  7. The bugs speed up the more you get right — stay sharp!

For teachers

What students practise

The alto clef version of Chameleon Note Snacks. Bugs fly across the staff and students name the note before the frog snaps them up. The start screen reminds students that the middle line is middle C, which is the anchor for reading everything else.

Using it in class

Great for viola players, or for any class exploring how the C clef works once they know treble and bass. Start on the Easy range (notes on the staff only) and move to Full once students are confident with the ledger lines.
