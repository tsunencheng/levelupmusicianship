Hi, I’m Tsun-En (Nami) Cheng.

I teach music to Years 7 to 9 in Western Australia. My teaching is Kodály-based, so my students sing, move and listen to music before they ever read it off a page. Outside school I write music for short films, play in a jazz big band, and I’m on the committees of ABODA WA and AJEA.

I want my students to walk out of a lesson feeling like they actually got better at something, not just that they finished a worksheet.

That’s where the name comes from. Most of the games here started as paper activities in my own classroom. I turned them into browser games so students could keep practising after the lesson was over. There are no accounts and nothing to install. Just open a game and play.

If you’re a music teacher and you use any of this with your classes, I’d love to hear how it goes. And if you’d like to help keep the site free, there’s a donate page.
