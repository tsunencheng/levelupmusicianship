Hi, I’m Tsun-En (Nami) Cheng.

I teach Years 7 to 9 music at Mount Lawley Senior High School, with a Kodály-informed approach, where through singing and ear work, musicianship skills can grow out of lived experience. Outside the classroom, I compose for short films, play in a jazz big-band and sit on the committee at ABODA WA and AJEA.

I want every student to leave a lesson feeling like they levelled up and not just like they finished a task.

That's where this site comes in. Every game here started as a real classroom activity, trialled with real students, then rebuilt as a browser game so the levelling-up feeling can travel beyond the one lesson it was made for. No accounts or installs needed, just open a page and play.

If you're a music teacher and something here is useful in your own classroom, please support me via the donate link to keep this site free.
